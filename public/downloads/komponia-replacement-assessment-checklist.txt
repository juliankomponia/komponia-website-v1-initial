KOMPONIA — PREPARE A REPLACEMENT ASSESSMENT
Version: 2026-10-07
General preparation checklist. Not a manufacturer datasheet, specification or offer.

Compare a proposed alternative with the actual requirement, current component and application-specific release criteria.

RECORD THE EXISTING CONFIGURATION
Identify the current part and document revision. Describe the function, installation and connected equipment. Separate a geometric reconstruction from a functional replacement task.
- Current part number and equipment reference.
- Relevant drawing, datasheet or sample.
- Reason for replacement and constraints on the change.

COMPARE AND DOCUMENT THE DIFFERENCES
Compare function, interfaces, operating conditions and documentation. A similar enclosure or part name does not establish suitability. Identify which differences need manufacturer clarification or evaluation.
Function — Does the alternative perform the required task?
Connections — Are inputs, outputs, cables and mechanical interfaces suitable?
Installation — Which dimensions, mounting and operating conditions apply?
Evaluation — Which differences need samples, checks or a new release decision?

AGREE THE RELEASE DECISION
Define the evaluation scope and who approves use in the intended application. Record the assessed configuration and any conditions for use; do not extend the decision automatically to another application.
