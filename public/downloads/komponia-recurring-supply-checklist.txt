KOMPONIA — PREPARE A RECURRING SUPPLY ARRANGEMENT
Version: 2026-10-07
General preparation checklist. Not a manufacturer datasheet, specification or offer.

Standard components and custom parts can both be included in an agreed delivery model. Begin with the actual product reference and demand.

DESCRIBE DEMAND AND ORDERING
Identify the required component version, expected consumption and the relevant planning period. Distinguish forecast quantities from committed orders or releases.
- Product reference and agreed version.
- Expected consumption and order quantities.
- Preferred delivery rhythm and documents.

DEFINE STOCK TERMS WHERE NEEDED
A proposed stock arrangement needs clear limits and commercial terms. Discuss ownership, financing, acceptance, ageing and treatment of unused or changed parts.
Quantities — Stock limits, release quantities and accepted commitments.
Ownership and financing — Who owns the stock and which terms cover its cost.
Changes — How revisions, ageing and discontinued items are handled.
Unused stock — Acceptance and disposition rules.

ASSIGN CHANGE AND REORDER RESPONSIBILITIES
Agree how a repeat order refers to the approved product and which changes need review. Additional sources need their own technical assessment and documented switching conditions.
