KOMPONIA — HOMEPAGE BRAND EXPRESSION
Date: 2026-10-19

User direction
Keep the existing product-led, Fictiv-informed homepage. The user likes its
structure and wants more Komponia personality, clearer separation and memorable
visual moments, drawing especially on the supplied LinkedIn banner concepts.
The guiding idea is a "30-year-old startup": curiosity and playful precision
combined with established technical experience. This is a design metaphor, not
a literal claim about Komponia's age. The factual experience reference is WTS
since 1997. This pass is implemented for review; final visual approval is pending.

What gives the page its own character
1. Product transformation is the signature interaction. The existing radial
   Komponia-to-real material reveal stays exactly as implemented. The surrounding
   sample stage is larger and uses a cropped outline of the actual brand mark,
   inspired by Minimal Industrial. The three existing process steps use blue,
   lavender and yellow according to their meaning.
2. Credibility has a clear visual anchor: a full-width Ink section with a large
   1997, explicitly labelled "WTS experience since", and the existing explanatory
   copy. No invented figures, customers, certifications or facilities.
3. Sourcing coordination has a diagram derived from Industrial Network: a blue
   pill, lavender quality module and yellow supply module connected by a single
   orthogonal route. HTML labels explain the three roles. No decorative global
   map or invented supplier locations.
4. Quality is a distinct lavender chapter with the existing expandable cards.
   Ongoing supply has a yellow editorial image frame. Blue remains the primary
   action colour, the new material still-life's ground and the closing band.
5. Selected section introductions use a split title/description arrangement,
   while the product grid, benefit cards, accordions, process tabs and section
   order remain. Keep a mix of quiet product content and concentrated brand
   moments; do not place the module motif behind every section.

Research and references, reviewed 2026-10-19
https://www.fictiv.com/
Actual homepage checked in the browser. Retain the strong product focus, clear
capability cards and controllable technical sections already used by Komponia.
Do not import its metrics, testimonials or network claims.

https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/interrogating-design-homogenization-in-web-vibe-coding/
Research examines how LLM-based website creation can narrow aesthetic diversity.
Our practical inference: derive decisions from the user's existing visual
language and content instead of accepting repeated default section patterns.

https://almanac.httparchive.org/en/2025/generative-ai#ai-colors
The Web Almanac discusses the "AI purple" association but finds no surge of the
specified colours within Tailwind sites and no statistically significant increase
in gradients, shadows or specific fonts. Its account does not support a reliable
single-colour AI detector. Do not reject Komponia's real lavender brand colour
because of the stereotype. The issue to address is visual interchangeability.

https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/
NN/g distinguishes useful product/context imagery from decorative images users
ignore. Our application: show the material and component clearly and keep
illustrative images distinct from evidence of actual company facilities.
