KOMPONIA / NEXT.JS HOMEPAGE
Art direction and implementation prompt
Prepared before homepage implementation on 17 October 2026

USER OBJECTIVE
The previous homepage looks too generic and too AI-generated. Rebuild the visual
experience using Next.js, Tailwind and shadcn/ui, following kill-ai-slop and the
Komponia design system. Research visual references first, write the full prompt,
then implement and inspect the result in the browser. This is a local repository
change and GitHub synchronization, not publication.

CREATIVE DIRECTION: ENGINEERING, MADE VISIBLE
Design the homepage as an industrial product editorial, with the confidence of
a well-designed component catalogue. Visitors should see the physical subject
before they read a service pitch. A large, isolated component photograph carries
the opening, not a dimmed office scene. Type establishes an unmistakable focal
point. Contrast comes from scale, proportion, light and dark, and the relationship
between a physical object and its explanation. The page must have its own rhythm.

The opening headline is “The part that fits.” Set it in Sora, sentence case,
solid Ink, in two deliberate lines. The supporting sentence explains the actual
business: component selection, custom OEM work and ongoing supply for equipment
manufacturers. Keep this explanation at a normal reading size. One blue primary
inquiry button and one quiet components link suffice. The hero image is large,
unobscured and recognizably industrial. Under the opening, offer three clear
paths: choose a component, develop an OEM part, plan ongoing supply. Use open
rows and thin rules, not three marketing cards with icons and matching copy.

COMPOSITION AND PAGE CONTENT
1. Navigation: one white header, original color wordmark, concise native links,
   blue inquiry action. A shadcn Sheet provides a keyboard-accessible mobile
   menu. Retain all existing routes in the site. No second sticky heading rail.
2. Opening: large two-line headline and component photograph, split unevenly.
   The image must not look like a small card. Keep the physical product whole
   where possible. Add one accurate WTS-origin sentence without a statistic row.
3. Task links: three open links, each describing a customer task. Direct paths
   to component catalogue, OEM and supply. No decorative ordinals or icons.
4. Component explorer: shadcn Tabs with four actual categories—controllers,
   sensors, heating, pressure/switching. Selecting a category changes a large
   product image, application explanation and the correct detail-page link.
   Keyboard navigation and touch must work. The first category is useful without
   interaction. These are illustrative category images, not saleable SKU claims.
5. Engineering: a large drawing/sample/finished-part image with an asymmetric
   text column. Explain in-house CAD, partner manufacturing, samples and an
   agreed series specification. A shadcn Accordion presents the real sequence
   without flooding the initial view. Supporting manufacturing routes are quiet
   links, not promoted as four equal primary businesses.
6. Komponia Lab: an Ink section with strong image/text contrast. Show the existing
   inspection illustration and explain dimensions, function and agreed batch
   sampling. Identify the scene as illustrative. No accreditation/certification
   claims. No invented outcomes or rejection rates.
7. Supply: express demand, agreed safety stock, call-offs and review as an actual
   sequence. Plain language and a useful visual relationship replace an invented
   dashboard. Commercial terms remain individually agreed. Include a direct
   supply inquiry link with the existing supported query parameter.
8. Closing: a short invitation to start with a drawing, part number or application.
   Inquiry link, confirmed email and telephone. The contact page still prepares
   a draft for the user's email application; never imply automatic transmission.
9. Footer: original wordmark, confirmed company identity/address and relevant
   routes. Information is grouped with alignment and whitespace, not panels.

DESIGN TOKENS AND DETAIL
Use local Sora throughout. Display 64–88px on desktop, 44–56px on small screens;
section headings 36–52px; body 16–18px; functional labels 12–14px. Headlines use
weight 500–600 and -0.03em tracking, not crushed extra-bold letterspacing. Keep
one typographic voice. No serif insertions, decorative monospace or colored words.
Blue #3E5AF5 is the action accent; hover #2A44D8. Ink #1F1F1D, white and neutral
surfaces dominate. Lavender #D4B9F3 and yellow #F7D44B remain restrained and earned
by brand imagery or a small inverse accent. No new palette.
Content width approximately 1200px, 40px wide gutters, 24px mobile. Major sections
have 96–120px space; closely related content uses much less. Do not apply one gap
everywhere. Use open regions, thin borders and very few card surfaces. Standard
media may keep the brand's 14px radius; full-bleed images do not need a frame.
Buttons remain pills. No resting shadows, floating badges, glass content cards,
continuous marquees, noise backgrounds, fake diagrams or cursor-following art.
Use the original logo assets; never redraw or typeset an approximate wordmark.

IMAGES AND MOTION
Use the strongest existing local component photographs and engineering scenes.
Keep all original image series and historical versions. Do not claim generated
illustrations show actual Komponia premises, employees, inspection records or
specific production products. Do not generate more media merely to add motion.
Higgsfield is a visual reference in this scope, not a paid generation order.
Transitions support actual state changes: tabs, accordion, menu. Keep them short
and eased. No automatic cycling is necessary to understand the page. Respect
prefers-reduced-motion. Avoid animation that makes the layout jump.

TECHNICAL DELIVERABLE
Use real Next.js App Router, native Link and routing, static generation where
appropriate, Tailwind utilities and genuine existing shadcn/ui components.
Preserve all existing subpages. Move legacy src/pages out of Next's reserved pages
directory. Scope legacy styles so they cannot override the new homepage.
Support old #/ URLs through a narrow compatibility redirect. Keep the confirmed
content/data and existing contact behavior. Serve local assets and fonts.
No fake progress dashboards, artificial counters or unimplemented controls.
