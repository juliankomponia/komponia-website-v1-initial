KOMPONIA — HOMEPAGE DESIGN CONCEPT
Date: 2026-10-19

Decision and source
The user likes the current homepage and approved the proposed
"Komponia-Feinschliff" after comparing a separate, reduced product-card sample.
The source is the supplied "Komponia Design System.zip", especially its
original brand sheet, palette, Sora typography and section-label treatment.
The source archive is reference material and remains unmodified. Its website
templates, placeholder claims and implementation instructions are not a mandate
to rebuild the current homepage.

Design direction
Keep a product-led industrial company website: generous product images, clear
technical hierarchy, concise English text and straightforward interactions.
The existing Fictiv-informed layout is the starting point. Express the Komponia
identity through typography, consistent colors and restrained repeatable details.
Do not revive previous homepage concepts or import the alternate UI kit.

Accepted website rules
- Sora, locally hosted, is the homepage typeface. Use 600 for principal headings,
  400 for body text and 500 for small labels. Preserve the current scale, content
  hierarchy and section order; adjust only where the new font needs room.
  The concept heading scales from 28 to 32px on narrow screens, keeping the
  existing three-line composition at 320px.
- Use #3E5AF5 for primary actions, interactive details and active navigation;
  #2A44D8 for hover, #1F1F1D for ink, #4C5057 for body text and #E1E3E8 for lines.
  White actions over dark hero imagery remain white for contrast.
- Section labels use small uppercase type, 0.18em tracking, a 36 x 2px rule and
  12px separation. At narrow widths use 0.16em, a 24px rule and 10px separation.
  Center labels in centered headings and align them left in feature sections.
  Use natural wrapping. Card category labels do not repeat the section rule.
- Capability cards keep the current image proportions, grid and 8-to-12 reveal.
  Use a fine border, 10px corner radius and no resting drop shadow. Existing
  image hover actions remain available. Buttons within homepage sections use
  restrained 6px corners. Pill controls from the source kit are not compulsory.
- Keep lavender restrained on existing inverse surfaces. Do not distribute
  different colors across peer cards merely for decoration. Yellow accents for
  supply and further image harmonization are possible later refinements, not
  implemented as part of this first pass.
- Preserve imagery and its illustrative descriptions. Do not turn generic
  illustrations into evidence of Komponia sites, machinery or customer products.

Implementation boundary
App's existing root has data-page="home" only at pathname "/". It provides the
font and shared header/footer color scope without changing the DOM relationship
needed for the hero's existing scroll accent. Global Tailwind tokens and subpage
styles remain untouched. No new wrapper, navigation routes or menu behavior.
Active files: src/pages/Home.tsx, src/pages/home-experience.css,
src/components/HomeExperience.tsx, src/components/ConceptToProduction.tsx.
The four hero slides, timing, focus/hover pause, radial component reveal, tabs,
accordions, capability expansion and content are preserved.
